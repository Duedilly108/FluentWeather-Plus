New in {PLUS}:

10-Day Hourly page. A "10-Day Hourly" button at the right of the Hourly title opens a page with every hour of the forecast, about 240 hours. A chart shows all 10 days, and a chart picker chooses temperature, feels like, precipitation, wind, gusts, humidity, UV index, cloud cover, visibility, dew point or pressure. A click on the chart scrolls the list to that hour.

The page has two views. List shows the main values of each hour, and a click on a row opens the other values. Table shows every value in its own column.

The Other forecast models card shows the range of the lows under the range of the highs.

In the settings panel, hovering over the name of each of the five Open-Meteo rows of "More data options" shows what the row adds and its source.
