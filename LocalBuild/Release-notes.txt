FluentWeather-Plus {PLUS}

A weather app for Windows with saved locations and weather icons in the taskbar. It is based on FluentWeather 2.1.0 by Gabboxl, with additions by Duedilly108.

Download FluentWeather-Plus-Setup-{PLUS}.exe below and double-click it to install. FluentWeather-Plus-{PLUS}.zip holds the same setup, the uninstaller and a README. FluentWeather-Plus-{PLUS}-SHA256.txt lists the SHA-256 hash of the setup file. Windows may ask for administrator approval once.

New in {PLUS}, compared with v51:

The sideways scroll bars under the saved locations, the detail cards, the days and the hours stay hidden until the mouse is over their row. A bar then fades in over 0.45 seconds, with an arrow at each end and a faint track. It is 3 points thick and sits 6 points under the buttons. It fades out 0.15 seconds after the mouse leaves.

Setup and the uninstaller accept /S for a silent run, for scripts and package managers. /SILENT, /VERYSILENT and /QUIET work the same way. No window appears and the exit code tells the result, 0 for success and 1 for failure. Setup does not start the app in this mode. /NOSTARTMENU and /NOSTARTUP turn off the Start menu items and the startup entry. FluentWeather-Uninstall.exe /S removes the app without a question, including the saved locations and settings.

When Open-Meteo is the data source, a day with expected thunder shows a bolt on its button and a line in its details. The thunder risk number needs weather.com, so it stays hidden. Earlier builds showed a fixed risk of 2 of 5 for these days.

The tray line "Next 12 h" also works when Open-Meteo is the data source. It has no ice amount in that case.

The publisher name of the app and of its uninstall entry is Duedilly108.
